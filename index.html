Settings & data protection

Back up your records, restore them, or export a spreadsheet

Your ride records are stored in this browser on this device using IndexedDB, with a local-storage compatibility copy. Back up regularly before clearing browser data, changing phones, or uninstalling a browser.
Backup includes ride records, expenses, and saved preferences.

Lyft and Uber are tracked through manual entry from your driver apps. This app does not sign in to either service, read their private APIs, or automatically import trips.

ESTIMATES ONLY · “Estimated net” subtracts fuel and the automatically calculated vehicle reserve. It is not tax advice or full business profit. It excludes insurance, taxes, platform adjustments, unpaid repositioning, financing and other costs unless you account for them separately. Online hours are entered manually. Use this tool only when safely parked.
